Uzor Privacy Policy

For Uzor 1.0 · Updated 2026-10-24

This policy describes the free version of Uzor. It does not cover Uzor-hosted AI, a service account or paid Pro services that are not provided in this version.

1. About Uzor and this policy

Uzor is a private notebook, journal, reading, flashcard and writing app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with an Apple Watch voice-capture companion.

Local features do not require a Uzor account. This version provides app features and iCloud sync free of charge, with a limit of 200 RSS feeds. It does not sell Pro or provide hosted AI credits. A provider you connect using your own API credentials may charge separately.

2. Data stored on your devices

Notes, journals, text, images, videos, recordings, reading files, feeds and saved articles, extracts, flashcards, review history, manuscripts and preferences are first stored on your device. Necessary identifiers and timestamps support saving, search, organization, reading and review. Recovery snapshots and limited diagnostic information are also stored locally.

Uzor has no feature that uploads your whole library to a developer-operated server. Optional iCloud sync, AI requests, online content retrieval and support correspondence involve the transfers described below. Local-first does not mean that every optional feature works without a network.

This version has no advertising SDK, advertising identifier access or developer-operated behavioral analytics upload. We do not sell data through this app or track people across apps for advertising.

3. iCloud sync

When you connect and use iCloud sync, workspace content, related preferences, change records and original attachments are sent through Apple CloudKit to the private database associated with your Apple account, and then to your other connected devices. A developer-operated server does not relay the library. The app keeps an account-scope identifier and sync baseline locally to distinguish accounts and resolve changes.

Apple handles its cloud service under its own terms and privacy policy. Uzor does not charge for sync, but your available iCloud storage, network and system conditions still apply.

Turning off sync does not erase existing cloud data. Uninstalling the app on one device does not automatically erase other devices, iCloud or exported backups. Conflict-recovery copies and deletion markers may remain to protect against accidental loss. Back up first and check available system iCloud storage controls, or contact support for help managing cloud content.

4. Audio, transcription and Apple Watch

Uzor records sound only after you start recording and grant microphone access. Phone and Mac recordings are saved locally. Watch recordings are first stored on the watch and transferred through WatchConnectivity to the paired iPhone when the system allows. After the phone acknowledges a durable copy, the watch attempts to remove the delivered audio while keeping delivery status. Undelivered or interrupted recordings may remain for recovery.

Received watch recordings appear in the appropriate day's journal. The iPhone can sync them through iCloud when it runs and meets sync conditions. Connections, scheduling and app state can delay delivery; immediate appearance on every device is not guaranteed.

When you request transcription, the app asks for speech-recognition authorization and requires supported on-device recognition for the selected language. It does not automatically fall back to server transcription. Unsupported requests leave the original recording intact. Audio included in iCloud sync or a backup can still leave the device through those separate paths.

5. Image recognition and your own AI API

Image/screenshot-to-card starts with on-device Apple Vision text recognition. This recognition step does not send the image to an AI provider. You can review and edit the recognized text before choosing to generate cards.

AI cards and digests are optional. Enter your HTTPS endpoint, model and API key in Settings → AI. Saving configuration makes no model request. Configuration and the key are stored in the current device's system Keychain; they are excluded from the library, Uzor backup exports and iCloud sync. Configure each device separately.

The generation screen shows the endpoint, model, selected material and a confirmation control. After confirmation, the app sends selected or entered text, source titles, internal source/evidence identifiers, coverage markers, language and task instructions directly to that endpoint. The API key is sent in the authorization header. The app does not automatically send your entire library, unselected sources, original screenshots or original audio. Personal information or URLs contained in selected text are included in that text.

Results are drafts for your review. Saved results enter the local library and can be synced according to your settings. You can avoid AI, cancel subsequent requests or remove the connection in Settings. Canceling local waiting cannot withdraw a request already received by a provider or guarantee that no charge occurs.

Presets currently include DeepSeek, MiniMax Mainland China, MiniMax Global and a custom compatible endpoint. Providers receive request and network information and may associate it with the provider account. Storage locations, retention and training or service-improvement practices depend on the service and account terms. Uzor does not promise zero retention or no training by every provider.

  • DeepSeek: Privacy policy and Open Platform Terms. Its policy describes storage in China, purpose-based retention and exceptions. The general policy includes training and improvement purposes; API scope, opt-outs and retention depend on applicable account terms. A consumer-product opt-out should not be assumed to cover every API request.
  • MiniMax Mainland China: Privacy policy and User Agreement. API content and authentication information support computation and troubleshooting. Its terms also describe algorithm improvement and encrypted, de-identified service optimization, statistics and security uses where individuals cannot be identified. Personal information from mainland operations is stored in China for necessary periods; backup deletion can take longer.
  • MiniMax Global: Privacy policy and Terms of Service. Its terms permit input/output processing to provide, maintain and improve services and for security. The policy describes US data-center storage, possible cross-border processing by affiliates or providers, and retention based on purpose and applicable law.
  • Custom endpoints: The address and service you choose determine the recipient, region and rules. Review its privacy and API terms before use.

Providers also process request times, models, call counts and token usage for metering, operation and usage statistics. Technical logs such as IP addresses, request identifiers and error statuses support security and troubleshooting. DeepSeek’s policy also describes approximate region inferred from IP for security checks. Uzor does not read GPS or send an advertising identifier to these providers.

These public documents do not provide Uzor with one fixed deletion period for all API requests. Authentication, request and security logs may be retained; specific account arrangements can differ. You can avoid external AI or remove unnecessary personal information before sending text.

6. RSS, web imports and link previews

Fetching an article, adding or refreshing RSS/Atom, or creating a link preview directly contacts the relevant website. That site and its network providers can receive the URL, IP address, request time and application network identifier. A personal token embedded in a feed URL is also sent to its destination.

Native text and metadata retrieval does not send website login cookies. Imported HTML becomes local text and its scripts are not executed. Remote preview images can separately contact the image host; this is a different request path. External links open in the system browser or appropriate app, which has its own practices.

Source sites may retain access logs. We cannot promise a retention period for arbitrary websites. You can use local imports and remove a feed to stop its subsequent refreshes. Unsubscribing does not automatically erase all saved articles or backups.

7. Permissions and choices

Microphone permission is needed for recording. Speech-recognition permission is requested for transcription. File/photo selection is used when you choose material to import; the app does not import unselected files. iCloud is used when you connect and use sync. Declining a permission prevents the related feature, not unrelated local editing.

Change microphone and speech permissions in system settings. Revocation does not recall data already transmitted; use Apple, provider or website controls for copies those services hold.

8. Support, diagnostics and the website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any device details, screenshots, logs or attachments you choose to send, for response and troubleshooting. Support mail is stored in Gmail. Google uses global infrastructure and may process mail outside your country or region; see Google’s privacy policy. Do not include passwords, API keys or unrelated private notes.

The app keeps a latest-startup diagnostic file locally, potentially containing errors, resource URLs and layout details. It has no automatic upload path to a developer server. Copies you provide by email or through Apple's feedback/diagnostic features are used for the related support work.

We use received support material only to respond, troubleshoot and handle the relevant requests. Routine support records are kept no longer than 12 months after resolution, with unrelated or unnecessary attachments deleted sooner. After verifying a deletion request, we will remove the mailbox records and working copies retained for that issue. If retention is legally required or necessary for an unresolved dispute, we will explain its scope and reason. Mail-service backup deletion remains subject to that service’s rules.

This support and privacy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Our pages are static documents without login, forms, advertising, behavioral analytics scripts or browser-preference storage. They do not receive libraries or API keys. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security and handles network-request and service-use information under its Privacy Statement.

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries, with retention determined by purpose and applicable law. The cited documents do not promise a fixed deletion period for all Pages access logs. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Website hosting operates separately from the app’s local library and iCloud sync.

9. Retention, deletion and requests

You can edit, export and delete content in the app and manage your exported files. Recovery snapshots reduce accidental loss: ordinary JSON snapshots rotate by count, currently up to 30; damaged-file recovery copies are separate. This is not a 30-day deletion promise. Deleting content does not immediately purge every snapshot, media file, other device or cloud copy.

Removing an AI connection deletes that local configuration/key, not provider records. Contact the privacy mailbox about access, correction or deletion of support data we hold. We will check the relationship between your request and the data to prevent unauthorized disclosure. Requests concerning third-party data should be sent to that party. There is no Uzor service account in this version to close.

We will reply to personal-information requests within 15 working days of receipt. If verification or completion needs more time, we will explain why and the next steps. This is a response period, not a promise that every third-party backup will be erased within that period.

10. Security, younger users and changes

System Keychain, app sandboxing, permissions and HTTPS AI requests help reduce unauthorized access. RSS and web sources may use HTTP. Device and network conditions affect security; no absolute guarantee is made. Protect your devices and backups and send only authorized material to endpoints you trust.

Uzor is not designed specifically for children and currently has no parental-control or age-verification system. Younger users should follow third-party age rules and obtain appropriate guardian guidance when using online services. Contact us about information you believe was inappropriately provided by a minor. An App Store age rating is not parental consent.

Material changes to features, recipients or processing will be reflected in this policy, with notice and consent where needed. Deferred hosted AI or paid services will receive separate disclosures if introduced; this policy does not authorize services that are not provided.
