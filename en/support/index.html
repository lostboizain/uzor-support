Uzor Support

For Uzor 1.0 · Updated 2026-10-24

Uzor brings notes, voice ideas, reading extracts and review cards into your personal library.

How do I capture an idea?

Open Notes → Journal to write in today's entry. Quick text and voice captures enter the journal; audio appears as a compact playback row with its duration and a transcription action. Archive helps organize collected material. Type [[Page name]] to connect related pages.

Is this version free?

Current app features and iCloud sync are free. You can add up to 200 RSS feeds; reaching the limit blocks new additions without removing existing feeds or saved articles. An older library already above the limit is not automatically trimmed.

This version does not sell Pro or include Uzor-hosted AI credits. Your own AI provider may charge under its plan, and iCloud capacity depends on your Apple account. This describes the current version, not a promise that every future service will be free forever.

How do my devices sync?

Use the same system iCloud account on iPhone, iPad and Mac, then check Settings → Sync in Uzor and enable the relevant sync options. Stay online, finish editing and return to Uzor while sync completes. “Saved locally” means the device has saved the content; it does not mean another device has received it yet.

If changes are delayed, check connectivity, iCloud space, the account on both devices and Uzor's sync status. Export a backup before major troubleshooting. Do not uninstall or delete cloud data as a first troubleshooting step. If a conflict appears, inspect both versions; keeping both can avoid overwriting important content.

Where are my Watch recordings?

Open Uzor on your paired Apple Watch, allow microphone access and start/finish a recording. It is saved on the watch first and transferred to iPhone when the system allows. It then appears in the appropriate day's journal. Open Uzor on iPhone and allow import and sync to complete before expecting it on other iCloud devices. You do not need to record again while the phone is unavailable; retain recordings that have not been delivered.

The Watch app captures audio only and does not browse notes. Connectivity, scheduling and app state can delay delivery. Saved on Watch and synced to all devices are different states.

Why is transcription unavailable?

Choose Transcribe on the audio row. It needs speech-recognition permission and supported on-device recognition for the device and language. If unavailable, the original audio remains; check permissions and system language resources before retrying. This version does not fall back to paid cloud transcription.

How do I make cards from a picture?

In AI cards, choose the image/screenshot action. Uzor recognizes text locally first. Correct the text, select the material, confirm the destination and generate drafts. Review questions, answers and citations before choosing which cards to save. Recognition and AI output can be wrong; generation does not automatically save every draft.

Why must I configure AI again on my phone?

Endpoints, models and keys are kept in each device's Keychain and are not synced through iCloud. Configure Settings → AI on each device with the correct region, model and key. Saving settings makes no model request. Compatible Chat Completions HTTPS endpoints are supported, but not every model or plan is guaranteed to work. Never send a key to support.

Generation sends the material and task information you confirm. Check the provider domain, model and selected material on the generation screen. Cancellation does not guarantee that the provider has not charged; use the provider's billing records for costs.

Why does a feed or article fail to import?

Use a publicly accessible RSS/Atom URL or article link. Login requirements, access restrictions, invalid formats or oversized text can prevent import. You can paste article text you are entitled to use. Some feeds provide summaries only; these are not represented as full articles.

Reading imports support PDF, EPUB, TXT, Markdown, HTML, DOCX, FB2 and some MOBI/AZW-family files. Encryption, DRM, layout variants and image-only ebooks may be unsupported. PDF keeps its original pages; scanned PDFs can be read but whole-book OCR is not promised. Retain original files and import only authorized material.

How do I back up or move my library?

Use the app's export/backup options to keep a complete backup before major changes. Text exports and full backups with attachments serve different purposes; verify that attachments open after migration. Sync is not a substitute for an independent backup.

Turning off sync, deleting content, uninstalling and clearing iCloud are separate actions. Recovery snapshots or other devices may still contain copies. Contact support before attempting a complete purge instead of repeatedly deleting and reinstalling.
